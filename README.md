# Honor of Kings Takeover

**gta5** × Honor of Kings · visual · idea by the team

Bring Honor of Kings into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned GTA V's airfield into a MOBA lane with crystal towers, hero ultimates and a dragon boss that crash-lands mid-fight.

## Install

Put the folder in your FiveM server's resources folder as sigf_mod, add "ensure sigf_mod" to server.cfg and join the server. Dawn and Dusk fighters, crystal towers and the Tyrant dragon appear on the map by themselves.
