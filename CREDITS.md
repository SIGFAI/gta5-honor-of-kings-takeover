# Credits

All new art, 3D models and sounds were generated with fal (um fal) for this mod; nothing was taken from Honor of Kings
or any other game, and no outside downloads were used.

- Crystal towers (Dawn and Dusk) and the Tyrant dragon: concept images by fal-ai/nano-banana-2, 3D models by fal-ai/trellis-2, converted to GTA V props with the kit's Blender + Sollumz tool.
- Hero crown and gold coin icons: fal-ai/nano-banana-2, background removed with fal BiRefNet.
- Announcer voice lines (First Blood, Double Kill, Triple Kill, Killing Spree, Victory, Tyrant): fal voice (text to speech).
- Sound effects (tower zap, ultimate, level up): fal sfx generation.
- Everything else uses GTA V's own peds, props, particles and sounds through FiveM.
